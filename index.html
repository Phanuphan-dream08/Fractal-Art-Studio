CHAPTER 1

Mathematical Foundations

Complex numbers provide the coordinate system. Recursive functions repeatedly transform z, while iteration and escape-time algorithms reveal structure from divergence.

CHAPTER 2

Fractal Geometry

Mandelbrot and Julia sets emerge from simple iterative rules. Their boundaries can show self-similar behavior, intricate detail and high structural complexity.

CHAPTER 3

Hausdorff Dimension

Hausdorff dimension generalizes ordinary dimension and can take non-integer values. For fractal boundaries it offers a mathematical way to quantify geometric complexity.

CHAPTER 4

GPU Computing

Fragment shaders map independent pixel calculations onto GPU execution. WebGL2 and GLSL provide a browser-accessible pipeline for parallel visual computation.

CHAPTER 5

Mathematical Art & Commercial Design

A mathematical pattern can become digital artwork, apparel graphics, packaging, posters and visual identity through a reproducible design workflow.
